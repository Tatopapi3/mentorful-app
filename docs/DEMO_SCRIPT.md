# Demo Day script: 5 minutes

Story: a school one year into Mentorful whose Culture Scan has fallen. A year ago it scored 85%. This year it is 70%, and tonight the principal finds out where and what to focus on next year.
Cast: **Eric** narrates. **Juan** plays the principal, **Mr. David**. **Bernard** plays **Ms. Afiya** (English). **Bertrand** plays **Mr. Stefano** (Science).
Follows the team's 5-minute guide: intros, then the problem, then the live demo, then the close.

The story's numbers belong to a sample school, so say "a school we'll call Pursuit" and keep "sample school" visible on the slide.
Both numbers are on screen: Pulse shows this year's scan (4.2 out of 6 for collective efficacy, 70%) with last year's (5.1, 85%) as an orange tick on each bar and "Down 0.9 since last year" above them. The scan is anonymous and has no departments: it shows what fell (Structural, down 1.4), and the Constellation shows where (English and Science, zero connections).

Screens: stage link, one tab, switch people with the bar at the bottom. Press **Reset** first. Five slides in all, at `/demo/slides` on the stage link (slide 5 is for questions). The first page of the stage link (`/demo`, the "show" tab) is the one the audience can see; the word-for-word script is on the "Script (for us)" tab, `/demo/script`.

## The script

Each person gets about 25 seconds for their intro (about 50 words); Eric gets about 40 because he also says what Mentorful is. Fill the brackets with your own words.

**ERIC (0:00, 40s)** *(Slide 1)*
"Hi everyone, we're the Mentorful team, building for a real client, a teacher-led startup founded by Mike and Nick. Their idea: the best coach for a teacher is the teacher down the hall. Schools pair teachers up, and a short survey, the Culture Scan, measures whether the staff is getting stronger together. I'm Eric. [one line about you, about 12 words]. I own privacy: no teacher can ever be singled out."

**JUAN (0:40, 25s)** *(Slide 1)*
"I'm Juan. For seven years I was a technical recruiter, matching people to the right teams. Outside work I'm a gamer and a sports fan, and I've always loved a good stat screen, because it shows who's improving. Pursuit is where I learned to build those myself. On Mentorful, I built the principal's dashboard."

**BERTRAND (1:30, 25s)** *(Slide 1)*

## The five slides

1. **Title and team:** "Evidence for principals. Privacy for teachers." and who is who.
2. **Pursuit, a year ago** (small label "Sample school"): a ring at 85% (5.1 out of 6); every teacher had a partner; the honest answers kept coming; private by design.
3. **This year, the scan fell** (small label "Sample school"): the score line from 85% to 70%; the four dimensions with last year's tick (Structural Low, down 1.4); the department map with English and Science unconnected; eight new teachers with no partner.
4. **Where to focus next year:** Structural; English and Science; next spring's scan is the proof. Ends on the closing line.
5. **Try it, and how it was built** (for questions, not narrated): a QR code to open the sample school on a phone, the tech stack and the timeline. Show it only after the close.

Open them at `/demo/slides` on the stage link (arrow keys to move, F for full screen).

## Timing and trimming

Every line has its own seconds, and they add up to 4:53. The speaking part of each is checked against a calm stage pace of about 130 words a minute, so a line that is too long for its seconds fails the tests. The brackets count as the number of words in their hint.
If you run long, cut in this order: the Trust page (keep the line), the "Tomorrow the board gets evidence" beat, Slide 3 (say its line over Slide 2), then the "[one skill you're proud of]" sentence in each intro.
Time it out loud once, with the clicks, before the staff dress rehearsal on Wed Nov 4 (presentations start at 6:50 PM, with 20 minutes of practice before). The final dress rehearsals are Sat Nov 7 and Sun Nov 8.

## Notes

- Do not say "no names anywhere". The faculty map in Reports shows names; the **deck** has none. Say "no teacher's name in the deck".
- The scan is anonymous and has no departments. Say the scan shows what fell and the map shows where; never say a department scored low.
- "Sample school" matters: the numbers are a story, and the app screens run on made-up data.
- Rehearse the switching. The bar at the bottom changes who you are in one click; do not open a second tab.
- Slide 5 (the QR code and how it was built) is for questions after the close. Do not show it while you present, so nobody looks down at a phone.
