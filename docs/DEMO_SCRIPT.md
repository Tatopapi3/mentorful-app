# Demo Day script: 5 minutes

Story: a school one year into Mentorful. A year ago its Culture Scan was under 50%. Tonight the principal closes the last gap.
Cast: **Eric** narrates. **Juan** is the principal. **Bernard** plays **Ms. Afiya** (English). **Bertrand** plays **Mr. Stefano** (Science).
Follows the team's 5-minute guide: intros, then the problem, then the live demo, then the close.

The story's numbers belong to a sample school, so say "a school we'll call Lincoln High" and keep "sample school" visible on the slide.
The 85% is on screen: the sample scan reads 5.1 out of 6 for collective efficacy. The "a year ago" numbers are in the story only (slide).

Screens: stage link, one tab, switch people with the bar at the bottom. Press **Reset** first. Four slides in all, at `/demo/slides` on the stage link.

## The script

**ERIC (0:00)**
"Hi everyone, we're the Mentorful team, and we built this for a real client, a teacher-led startup founded by Mike and Nick. Their idea is simple: the best coach for a teacher is the teacher down the hall. Schools pair teachers up, and a short survey, the Culture Scan, measures whether the whole staff is getting stronger together. I'm Eric, and I own privacy: no teacher can ever be singled out."

**JUAN (0:40)**
"I'm Juan, a former technical recruiter. I built the principal's dashboard."

**BERNARD (0:50)**
"I'm Bernard. [one line about you]. I built the teacher dashboard."

**BERTRAND (1:00)**
"I'm Bertrand. [one line about you]. I built pairing and the pair chat."

**ERIC (1:10)** *(Slide 2)*
"Picture a school we'll call Lincoln High. A year ago its Culture Scan came back under fifty percent. Teachers who shared a hallway had never spoken. Most said they would not admit a mistake to a colleague. The principal wanted to help, but the moment she asked, the honest answers stopped."

**ERIC (1:30)** *(Slide 3)*
"So the school adopted Mentorful. It pairs teachers across departments, and what they write stays private by design. Tonight, the principal is Juan, one year later."

**JUAN (1:45)** *(Pulse, then Constellation)*
"This year's scan: five point one out of six. Eighty-five percent. And see this? Any group under five, I can't see. Not because I didn't click. Because I'm not allowed to. But one thing still keeps me up." *(Constellation)* "English and Science. Same building, same kids. Zero connections."

**JUAN (2:10)** *(Pairing, Draft new round)*
"So I let Mentorful draft a round across departments. One click." *(the map draws the new links)* "English and Science, connected. I still make the call. Approved." *(Approve)*

**ERIC (2:30)**
"One of those new pairs: Ms. Afiya in English and Mr. Stefano in Science. A month ago they had never spoken. Today they spent twenty minutes on essays written by AI."

**BERNARD, as Ms. Afiya (2:45)** *(switch to Ms. Afiya, Log a conversation, Save draft, Submit)*
"That was the most useful conversation I've had all semester. Let me log it. Ten seconds. And this note? Only I can read it."

**BERTRAND, as Mr. Stefano (3:05)** *(switch to Mr. Stefano, open Chat)*
"Sending you my rubric tonight. Same time next week?"

**ERIC (3:20)**
"So, principal. What do you see?"

**JUAN (3:25)** *(switch to Juan, Pulse, then Trust)*
"One more logged conversation. But what they said? I can't read a word of it." *(Trust page)* "I can count it. I can't read it. That's why teachers tell the truth."

**JUAN (4:00)** *(Reports)*
"Tomorrow I walk into the board meeting with evidence: who is connected, and a deck with no teacher's name in it. One click, a PDF."

**ERIC (4:25)** *(Slide 4)*
"A year ago, under fifty percent. Today, eighty-five. Two teachers who were strangers are coaching each other, the principal has proof, and nobody had to feel watched. Evidence for principals. Privacy for teachers. This is Mentorful. Thank you."

## The four slides

1. **Title and team:** "Evidence for principals. Privacy for teachers." and who is who.
2. **Lincoln High, a year ago** (small label "Sample school"): Culture Scan under 50%; English and Science, zero conversations; most staff would not admit a mistake to a colleague.
3. **What the school adopted:** pairs across departments; private by design; groups under 5 hidden.
4. **A year later:** under 50% to 85%, and the closing line.

Open them at `/demo/slides` on the stage link (arrow keys to move, F for full screen).

## Timing and trimming

About 400 spoken words, plus your two one-line intros (roughly 440): about 3:10 of talking and 1:40 of clicking, so the last line lands near 4:50. Time it out loud once; the clicks are the part that runs long.
If you run long, cut in this order: the "Tomorrow I walk into the board meeting" beat (Juan, 4:00), then the Trust page, then each person's one-line intro.

## Notes

- Do not say "no names anywhere". The faculty map in Reports shows names; the **deck** has none. Say "no teacher's name in the deck".
- "Sample school" matters: the numbers are a story, and the app screens run on made-up data.
- Rehearse the switching. The bar at the bottom changes who you are in one click; do not open a second tab.
