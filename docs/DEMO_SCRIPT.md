# Demo Day script: 5 minutes

Story: a school one year into Mentorful. A year ago its Culture Scan was under 50%. Tonight the principal closes the last gap.
Cast: **Eric** narrates. **Juan** is the principal. **Bernard** plays **Ms. Afiya** (English). **Bertrand** plays **Mr. Stefano** (Science).
Follows the team's 5-minute guide: intros, then the problem, then the live demo, then the close.

The story's numbers belong to a sample school, so say "a school we'll call Lincoln High" and keep "sample school" visible on the slide.
The 85% is on screen: the sample scan reads 5.1 out of 6 for collective efficacy. The "a year ago" numbers are in the story only (slide).

Screens: stage link, one tab, switch people with the bar at the bottom. Press **Reset** first. Four slides in all, at `/demo/slides` on the stage link. The first page of the stage link (`/demo`, the "show" tab) is the one the audience can see; the word-for-word script is on the "Script (for us)" tab, `/demo/script`.

## The script

Each person gets about 25 seconds for their intro (about 50 words); Eric gets about 40 because he also says what Mentorful is. Fill the brackets with your own words.

**ERIC (0:00, 40s)** *(Slide 1)*
"Hi everyone, we're the Mentorful team, building for a real client, a teacher-led startup founded by Mike and Nick. Their idea: the best coach for a teacher is the teacher down the hall. Schools pair teachers up, and a short survey, the Culture Scan, measures whether the staff is getting stronger together. I'm Eric. [one line about you, about 12 words]. I own privacy: no teacher can ever be singled out."

**JUAN (0:40, 25s)** *(Slide 1)*
"I'm Juan. For seven years I was a technical recruiter, matching people to the right teams. Outside work I'm a gamer and a sports fan, and I've always loved a good stat screen, because it shows who's improving. Pursuit is where I learned to build those myself. On Mentorful, I built the principal's dashboard."

**BERNARD (1:05, 25s)** *(Slide 1)*
"I'm Bernard. Before Pursuit, I [what you did and where you're from, about 9 words]. What got me into building was [the moment or reason, about 7 words]. At Pursuit I learned to [one skill you're proud of, about 5 words]. On Mentorful, I built the teacher dashboard, the part teachers use every week."

**BERTRAND (1:30, 25s)** *(Slide 1)*
"I'm Bertrand. Before Pursuit, I [what you did and where you're from, about 8 words]. What pulled me in was [the moment or reason, about 7 words]. At Pursuit I learned to [one skill you're proud of, about 5 words]. On Mentorful, I built pairing and the pair chat. Eric, take it away."

**ERIC (1:55, 18s)** *(Slide 2)*
"Picture a school we'll call Lincoln High. A year ago its Culture Scan came back under fifty percent. Teachers who shared a hallway had never spoken, and the honest answers stopped the moment the principal asked."

**ERIC (2:13, 11s)** *(Slide 3)*
"So the school adopted Mentorful: pairs across departments, and what teachers write stays private. Tonight, the principal is Juan, one year later."

**JUAN (2:24, 26s)** *(Switch to Juan. Point at the Culture Scan and the privacy badge. Open Constellation.)*
"This year's scan: five point one out of six. Eighty-five percent. Any group under five, I can't see, because I'm not allowed to. But one thing still keeps me up. English and Science. Same building. Zero connections."

**JUAN (2:50, 20s)** *(Click Draft new round. The map draws the new links. Click Approve 4 pairs.)*
"So I let Mentorful draft a round. One click. English and Science, connected. I still make the call. Approved."

**ERIC (3:10, 10s)** *(Ms. Afiya's screen)*
"Ms. Afiya in English, Mr. Stefano in Science. Strangers a month ago. Today, twenty minutes on essays written by AI."

**BERNARD, as Ms. Afiya (3:20, 18s)** *(Switch to Ms. Afiya. Log a conversation, write a line, Save draft, Submit this entry.)*
"That was the most useful conversation I've had all semester. Let me log it. Ten seconds. And this note? Only I can read it."

**BERTRAND, as Mr. Stefano (3:38, 10s)** *(Switch to Mr. Stefano. Open Chat, send the message.)*
"Sending you my rubric tonight. Same time next week?"

**ERIC (3:48, 4s)** *(Mr. Stefano's screen)*
"So, principal. What do you see?"

**JUAN (3:52, 26s)** *(Switch to Juan. Show Pulse (logged conversations 9 to 10), then the Trust page.)*
"One more logged conversation. But what they said? I can't read a word of it. I can count it. I can't read it. That's why teachers tell the truth."

**JUAN (4:18, 14s)** *(Open Reports. Show the faculty map, then Download PDF for the deck.)*
"Tomorrow the board gets evidence, and a deck with no teacher's name in it. One click, a PDF."

**ERIC (4:32, 20s)** *(Slide 4)*
"A year ago, under fifty percent. Today, eighty-five. Two strangers are coaching each other, the principal has proof, and nobody had to feel watched. Evidence for principals. Privacy for teachers. This is Mentorful. Thank you."

## The four slides

1. **Title and team:** "Evidence for principals. Privacy for teachers." and who is who.
2. **Lincoln High, a year ago** (small label "Sample school"): Culture Scan under 50%; English and Science, zero conversations; most staff would not admit a mistake to a colleague.
3. **What the school adopted:** pairs across departments; private by design; groups under 5 hidden.
4. **A year later:** under 50% to 85%, and the closing line.

Open them at `/demo/slides` on the stage link (arrow keys to move, F for full screen).

## Timing and trimming

Every line has its own seconds, and they add up to 4:52. The speaking part of each is checked against a calm stage pace of about 130 words a minute, so a line that is too long for its seconds fails the tests. The brackets count as the number of words in their hint.
If you run long, cut in this order: the Trust page (keep the line), the "Tomorrow the board gets evidence" beat, Slide 3 (say its line over Slide 2), then the "[one skill you're proud of]" sentence in each intro.
Time it out loud once, with the clicks, before the dress rehearsal on Sat Nov 7.

## Notes

- Do not say "no names anywhere". The faculty map in Reports shows names; the **deck** has none. Say "no teacher's name in the deck".
- "Sample school" matters: the numbers are a story, and the app screens run on made-up data.
- Rehearse the switching. The bar at the bottom changes who you are in one click; do not open a second tab.
